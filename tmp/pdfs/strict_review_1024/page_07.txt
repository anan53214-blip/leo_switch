Task feedback is assigned when an outcome is resolved. For
local-only execution, the reserved completion time determines
the outcome at dispatch; remote outcomes are evaluated upon
MEC completion, expiration, or termination. Interruption,
association-failure, and load-balance feedback are assigned in
the corresponding slot. Thus, the replay reward follows (29),
including outcomes from previously dispatched workloads.
